Design rationale. Every mark sits on a hexagonal tile drawn as three faces — bright top, mid left, dark right — so it reads as a small extruded object under a light. The vendor's real colour fills the top face; a white glyph sits on top; the outer bevel inherits the card's accent via currentColor. Result: dimensional, technical, and unmistakably that vendor — no generic robot or mascot reinterpretation.
